## 🔴 03 — Advanced
> *End-to-end commercial analytics & fintech deliverables*

### [Remittance Payment Economics Model](./03_Advanced/Payment_Economics_Model.xlsx)
> **Tools:** Advanced Excel (VLOOKUP, SUMPRODUCT, sensitivity stress-testing) · Python (numpy, data modeling)  
> *   Designed a professional payments model for a fintech remittance context, specifically modeled on **ACE Money Transfer's** international UK/Europe-to-Pakistan corridors (GBP-PKR, USD-PKR, EUR-PKR).
> *   Generated a **1,000-row transactional synthetic audit log** utilizing lognormal distributions to model send sizes and status rates (2.1% failed rate).
> *   Built dynamic **Tiered Card Acquirer Fee lookups** and calculated **Customer LTV (based on contribution margin), LTV:CAC, and Payback periods**.
> *   Structured a **Revenue Leakage Tracker** waterfall isolating card costs, compliance (£0.35/tx), payout (£0.28/tx), failed gateways, FX slippage (0.15%), and promotional waivers (0.3%).
> *   Developed a polished **Executive Dashboard** with KPI cards, multi-axis volume vs. margin combo charts, and traffic-light corridor health indexes (gridlines disabled).

### [FMCG Margin Compression & Sensitivity Model](./03_Advanced/FMCG_Margin_Compression_Model.xlsx)
> **Tools:** Excel (Zebra-striping, dynamic conditional formatting, custom visual charts)  
> *   Built a professional financial model based on a 90-observation panel dataset of 15 PSX-listed Pakistani FMCG companies (2019-2024).
> *   Constructed a **Summary Dashboard** walking through revenue, COGS pressure, and average GPM over time, with active conditional formatting (red when GPM < 25%).
> *   Developed a **CMAM (Contribution Margin After Marketing) Sensitivity Matrix** assessing ad spend shifts (±10%, 20%, 30%).
> *   Built a **Floating Stacked Column Waterfall Chart** visualizing GPM erosion from a 26.2% base (2019) to a 23.9% inflation low (2022) and subsequent 2023 pricing-power recovery.

### [FMCG Inflation Impact Power BI Dashboard](./03_Advanced/FMCG_Inflation_Impact_Dashboard.pbix)
> **Tools:** Power BI Desktop (DAX, scatter plots, trend lines, multi-page data stories)  
> *   Designed a structured **5-page C-suite interactive dashboard suite** analyzing marketing response during Pakistan's peak inflation cycle.
> *   Tabs Renamed to Professional Terminology:
>     1. *1. Macroeconomic Baseline* (CPI & SBP Policy Rate timeline overlay)
>     2. *2. A&P Expenditure Dynamics* (average COGS ratio vs. advertising-to-sales ratios)
>     3. *3. GPM vs. A&P Correlation* (scatter-plot regression proving the absence of sector correlation, $\beta = -0.110, p > .10$)
>     4. *4. Cross-Firm Benchmarking* (side-by-side comparative ranking with year slicers)
